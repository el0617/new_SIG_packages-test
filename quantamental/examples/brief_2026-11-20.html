Limitations to say out loud

  • Macro data use publication-lag rules, not ALFRED vintages, so revisions are not modelled.
  • Benchmark weights are held at their 2026 values; sector history before an ETF existed uses proxies (IYR, SPY).
  • Revision and valuation signals need Bloomberg exports and are skipped without them.
  • Four US recessions since 1985 is a small sample for any recession model.
